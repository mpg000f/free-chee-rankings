    How the model works

    The top eight qualify. Seeds are sorted by winning percentage, then cumulative points for. A tied game counts as half a win. If both record and points for are identical, the display orders those teams alphabetically by owner and the simulation randomly breaks the tie.

    The odds are estimates from 10,000 simulated seasons. It draws independent weekly scores using each team’s full-season average, pulled toward the league average by four games’ worth of weight, and a shared measure of weekly scoring volatility. When you pick a winner, the model only uses score draws where that team wins. Entered scores stay fixed. Standings use the average score from draws consistent with each projected result.

    The Yahoo scoreboard refresh is scheduled daily. In-progress games use Yahoo’s projected final scores when available, with uncertainty scaled down as points come in. The simulation treats points already scored as a floor. For extreme upsets, a forced winner may be assigned the minimum score needed to win.
