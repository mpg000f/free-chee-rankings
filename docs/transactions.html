How This Is Scored

A player's value here is the fantasy points he produced while on that manager's roster, from the week he arrived onward. Drop him and the counter stops, which is the honest read — the move stopped paying at that point.

Trade grades compare what a side received against what it sent away, per week left in the season, so a Week 12 swap isn't judged on the same scale as a Week 2 one. The bands are symmetric: in a two-team deal, one side's A is the other's F, and an even trade lands both sides on C.

One caveat worth stating: Yahoo's weekly roster data records no lineup slot, so points scored on the bench count the same as points that won a matchup. A stashed breakout still shows up as a great pickup even in the weeks he never started.
