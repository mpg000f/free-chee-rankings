The top of the list is not a surprise, but the shape of it is. Nick Wright and Powder are separated by 1.4 points and built the same way: a quarterback, a receiver and a back all over 74. Darkness is third here and first in the actual standings, because his fourth, fifth and sixth-best players are better than anybody else’s.

The names that do not belong are the story. Free Paid’s big three are three quarterbacks, one of whom is on the bench most weeks. The Art of the Deal is ninth on this list and dead last in points, which means the gap between Bryce Young, Jordan Love and the rest of that roster is a canyon. Shough and F#ck has a defense in its top three. Pigs on the 7th Rank has a kicker, and is 3-1 anyway which is the clearest proof available that depth beats star power over a month.

At the bottom, Ginny Sack is last at 163.0 despite Javonte Williams being a genuinely good player, and Stewed C’s best three are a quarterback, a tight end and a back he picked up along the way. Both rosters are asking four or five players to be slightly above average every week, which is a difficult way to live.
