Ready boys? After one week of football, it’s becoming clear that the committee is close to perfecting its craft. Using no models, data, or algorithms in the preseason rankings yielded some elite results for week one. While other analysts spent August chasing Mickey Mouse narratives and listening to anonymous training camp reports, the committee relied on intuition, gut feeling and personal bias. The results speak for themselves. Preseason #1 Boone led the league in scoring in week one. Preseason #16 Work Ass finished last. When comparing preseason rank to projected week one points, the relationship is undeniable. The graph below shows a clear correlation between points scored and preseason rank. Even more so if you remove a couple of the outliers from the dataset. Detractors may attempt to distract from this achievement by mentioning Art of the Deal or Hello Darkness My Old Friend. The committee considers those examples statistically insignificant and questions why anyone would focus on two isolated data points when the broader trend so clearly supports our expertise. One week is obviously far too small a sample to reconsider any of our preseason opinions, but it is more than enough evidence to declare the opinions we got right completely validated. Now without further ado.
