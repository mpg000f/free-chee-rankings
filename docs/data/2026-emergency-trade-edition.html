Emergency Trade Edition

Stewart C and Boone executed a double deal this week, one that had much of the league judging Stewart pretty harshly. The Committee itself had initial questions about collusion, some sort of secret deal to give Stewed extra cash at the draft next year or perhaps an off-the-books benefit like a fat bag being delivered to Connecticut. Let’s get into the details.

Trade One

Boone ReceivesStewed C Receives
Jayden DanielsJaylen Waddle
Puka NacuaD’Andre Swift
—Tanner McKee

Now this trade occurred on Tuesday, November 3rd, around midday. Perhaps Stewed was concerned about the early election results? Our insiders have known for quite some time he was a stealth contributor to the campaign of Roy Cooper in North Carolina. Of course he had nothing to worry about as Cooper cruised to a victory to secure a Senate seat for the Democrats in North Carolina. However, our hypothesis is that Stewed’s distraction as he quaked in his boots about potential election losses allowed Boone to pull one over on him. Boone, of course, would never care about something so trivial as election results. That’s why he’s among the greatest managers of our time.

Now in a vacuum this deal is bad for Stewed. He gives up a top-five receiver and a top-12 QB for a good running back (probably top 15 rest of the season), Jaylen Waddle (mental retard tier) and Tanner McKee. McKee has been functional and deserves credit for that, but McKee is a massive downgrade from Jayden. If you’re a Waddle believer (the Committee despises him and everything he stands for), maybe you could pitch this deal as acquiring three starters for two. But even then we would counter that D’Andre Swift is not enough to compensate for downgrading from Jayden to McKee and Puka to Waddle. We don’t like to pile on our good friend Stewed, but this was not a deal the Committee would have made. Credit Boone, of course, for having the wherewithal to know Stewed would be vulnerable to an emotional decision on election day.

If only it stopped here, perhaps Stewed would not be the new favorite for relegation, but fate had other ideas.

On Wednesday, November 4th, Howie Roseman, Nick Sirianni and the ever-important Big Dom decided they’d had enough of Tanner McKee and benched him while announcing Joe Milton “Friedman” as the Eagles’ starting quarterback for this week. This of course sent Stewed into a panic as he realized he’d shipped out Puka and Jayden for Waddle, Swift and a backup QB.

We don’t have a firm timeline on this, but from what our investigative reporters have been able to gather, Stewed reached back out to Boone about another deal that night and negotiations went back and forth all through the night and into the morning until they agreed to another deal.

Trade Two

Boone ReceivesStewed C Receives
D’Andre SwiftTyler Allgeier
Marcus MariotaJoe Milton

In a vacuum, the Committee finds this deal much more defensible. Jeremiyah Love (also on Stewed’s team) is out for at least a month and Allgeier is expected to be a bellcow in his place. Plus he gets the Eagles’ actual starting QB in Joe Milton. Now what many don’t know is that Stewed has long had a soft spot for Joe Milton. Going all the way back to COVID summer when Milton was preparing to be a first-year starter at Michigan, Stewed was known to work Milton hype into his spews from time to time. In one memorable episode, he was talking Wheatley’s ear off about this rocket-armed cyborg that Jim Harbaugh was prepared to start at Michigan that fall. Unsurprisingly, Wheatley’s eyes quickly glazed over. Whether that was due to boredom or the cocktail of substances he’d cheffed up for himself that day nobody knows. In any event, Stewed got his guy in Milton. He traded for a monopoly on the Eagles QB situation in return for a downgrade at running back. The Committee can buy that.

On the flip side, Boone picked up insurance for Jayden Daniels in the form of Marcus Mariota and got back D’Andre Swift. Elite move for him as well. Swift has been a truck in Chicago’s offense and this is a clear upgrade for him over Allgeier, improving his starting lineup while sacrificing a QB he was never going to play anyway. Credits in the chat for Boone.

The Aggregate

Now this is where it gets worse. Let’s look at this stuff in aggregate.

Boone swapped Philly’s dogshit QB room for Washington’s pretty good one while trading in Waddle (slop merchant) and Allgeier (solid but unspectacular player) for Puka fucking Nacua. Meanwhile, Stewed all but assured he’ll be the favorite for relegation heading into Week Nine.

The Committee sat around last night thinking of trades to compare this one to. Our first thought was the Luka Dončić trade from a couple years ago, but no, that’s too simple. Maybe the Russell Wilson for picks and players trade that sent him to Denver? What a disaster that was. But nope, that wasn’t the comp either.

The perfect comp was the 2007 trade between the Marlins and Tigers that sent Miguel Cabrera and Dontrelle “D-Train” Willis to Detroit for six players who gave Florida very little to show for it. Those six were Cameron Maybin, Andrew Miller, Burke Badenhop, Mike Rabelo, Dallas Trahern and Eulogio De La Cruz. Miller eventually became nasty, which was lovely news for teams other than the Marlins.

Much like the Marlins could point to getting a number of guys back for a couple of more established stars, Stewed can point to the depth he got back in return for Puka and Jayden. Boone, of course, is hoping this deal translates into a championship, unlike the Miggy deal for Detroit. Somewhere Dave Dombrowski is wondering if he could’ve gotten the Marlins to send him a quarterback too.

So in summary, Stewed got fleeced. The Committee believes he undervalued his superstars and overvalued the borderline replacement-level players that he got in return. Boone is positioning himself back among the top contenders after falling slightly due to some recent injuries. The Committee hears it from our boots on the ground that the newest resident of Weehawken was damn near apoplectic hearing the news. He began to babble about collusion, skullduggery and the ethics of the league. A passerby reportedly even saw him emulating the famous Triple H gif. A tier of title contenders that seemed to be narrowing to Deez, Zaukas and TK now must welcome another into their ranks.

The Committee doesn’t like to pile on, especially when it comes to our friend Stewed, who’s had some bad luck over the course of this league. But we can only call it like we see it.
