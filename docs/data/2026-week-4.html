September Awards

September MVP: Josh Allen Nick Wright spent big to get the #1 player in fantasy but as always he is delivering. He put up 36 and 41 the first two weeks before a merely human total of 19 in Week 3. Even with Tua as his QB2, Allen has done enough to carry Nick Wright to a 2-1 start.
Best Draft Pick: Christian Watson $7 Credit the committee for knowing Boone killed the draft at the time. He found value all over the place but none bigger than Christian Watson. Watson is the WR3 through three weeks behind only JSN and ASB. He’s producing big time for a terrible Packers team and the committee only expects it to continue.
Worst Draft Pick: Drake Maye $55 Could it be anyone else? A year after being the breakout star of the league and finishing as the QB2, his brain has melted and the haters from North Willow to Beijing to Tel Aviv are having an absolute ball watching it. Maye is currently outside the top-30 in QB points through three weeks. If this continues, it will be quite possibly the worst pick in the history of the league that wasn’t injury related. People forget Mikey spent $77 on McCaffrey in 2024 and he literally didn’t play.
Owed an Apology: Zaukas Ranked 11th in the preseason, the committee believed history would repeat itself but we couldn’t have been more wrong. Last week some argued it was unsustainable so this week he dropped 142 and told Shough to suck his sausage as he moved to 3-0. The committee will be sending him an Arby’s gift card.
September Fraud: Shough and F#ck This was almost as much of a slam dunk as Maye for worst pick. Jahmyr Gibbs has been so absurdly good that it’s nearly impossible to expect it to continue. He is on pace to outscore LaDainian Tomlinson’s all-time points record by 100 points. If he breaks the record by more than 100 points, the committee chairman promises at least one shaved head. Additionally, Tyler Shough is just throwing the ball a million fucking times. Admittedly he has been better than we expected but there’s simply no way this volume continues. If his current pace keeps up, he would end up with the 4th most pass attempts of any player in NFL history over the course of the season. Fraudulent stuff.
Schedule Victim of the Month: Phenom Justin sits 1-2 while scoring the sixth-most points in the league, and his all-play record of 28-17 is identical to the unbeaten Boone. He has faced 373 points, third-most in the league, including a Week 2 loss in which he scored 127.9, a number that would have beaten twelve of the other fifteen teams that week. Nothing is wrong here except the calendar.
