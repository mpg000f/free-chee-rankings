Boone

Boone retains his perch for the third straight edition of the rankings. It was a true team effort as nobody scored more than 18 points but he had seven guys in double figures. As one committee member put it, it was almost like when you have a 2K team of all 82s like the 2013 Nuggets. Boone’s pure roster talent is far greater than those Nuggets though. Baker is playing like the fry cook at Grubbs and yet Boone probably still has one of the better QB pairings to go with King Henry and a vaunted trio at receiver. Christian Watson and Parker Washington especially look poised to significantly outperform their auction value after two weeks. The bench is definitely shallower than we expected in the preseason, but given the wealth elsewhere it’s a minor concern for now.

Stock Up: Kaimi Fairbairn. Some might think the committee only brings up Kaimi so we could launch a chorus of “KAIMI” shouts in the committee room. And while that’s an obvious side benefit, there’s a serious case that he’s an undervalued asset for Boone. If Stroud and the Texans offense continue to sputter, Kaimi’s bionic leg will prove to be essential for booming kicks.
Stock Down: Marvin “William Henry” Harrison. Much like William Henry Harrison holds the record for shortest tenure of any American president, MHJ will soon hold the record for biggest generational bust in the history of receivers. Everyone from Mel Kiper to Emmanuel Macron to Boyle was treating this guy like the second coming of his father, when in reality he’s much closer to Ramses Barden.
