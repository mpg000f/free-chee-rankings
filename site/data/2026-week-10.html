Deadline week. Kirk Cousins is a Viking again. Fernando Mendoza is a starting quarterback. Josh Downs is black Wes Welker.

These rankings reflect results through Week 9, when Rhino scored 67.70 and briefly made everyone else feel better about their teams. This week’s feature is the Deadline Deal: what the committee would do with your roster if any of you returned our calls.

Tier One: Buyers

1
🔥Paul
Hello Darkness My Old Friend
—

Darkness is 8-1 and leads the league with 1,196.58 points. There is no scheduling argument to make here. Burrow, K9, McBride and this ragtag crew keep Darkness in the driver’s seat even after quite a week of player movement. He’s so confident he even invited his chief competition into his facility this past weekend and gave them a tour of the state-of-the-art training room. Let’s just say the technology has come a long way from using frozen peas to ice his knees.

Joe Burrow’s explosiveness over the past month has proven to be a competitive advantage we didn’t see coming. He and Josh Allen are now both averaging 25.7 points per game which would slot them into 5th since 2000 if they can keep that pace. Only 2019 Lamar, 2011 Rodgers, 2018 Mahomes and 2013 Peyton have exceeded 25.7 PPG over a full season. That type of production to pair with his elite running game is a real separator for Zaukas HQ.

Deadline Deal: Nothing. Reports out of South Florida suggest he was practically laughing on the phone when people called trying to make a deal. The level of confidence this front office has right now is through the roof. It almost makes you wonder if Brian Cashman is on staff.

Boone
▲ 4

Up from ninth to third, Boone is 6-3 with a league-low 900.54 points against. He has made excellent use of the schedule Yahoo provided. Obviously, we did an emergency assessment of last week’s acquisitions. Now we need to pour one out for Puka Nacua as he’s going to miss a month with a knee injury. We wonder who Boone will keep on the pine once bye weeks are over and he has his full arsenal (minus Puka) to attack each week. Something the committee will ponder.

Deadline Deal: Sell MHJ. Maybe somebody still believes in this guy? Quite frankly one of the biggest busts in modern sports history. He hasn’t had more than five targets or 40 yards in any game this year. Gallo made a convincing case that OG Thing with Hair, known for roaming 1st street, could exceed 40 yards in an NFL game.

Shough and F#ck

Fourth in scoring but we’re not really sure how. We look at this roster and see Gibbs who is of course elite but after that? Tuten good player. Shough solid. It really makes you think about the good old times back in August when Shough legitimately thought his combination of Herbert and Shough was better than the dragon-breathing tandem of Trevor Lawrence and Patrick Mahomes. Among player-seasons with at least 13 games played, Herbert is on pace to finish as one of the three worst draft values in our model, alongside 2023 Dalvin Cook and 2024 Blake Corum. Can you imagine?

Deadline Deal: He should probably stand pat. He needs to be better at lineup decisions. Leaving KC Concepcion on his bench this week cost him the win. That one small change and he’s 5-4 in 7th place right now.
