Waiver Wire

Everything added off the wire after Week 1, ranked by what it went on to score for the manager who grabbed it. Trades are graded separately on the Trade Grades page.

Swipe →

Points are what the player produced while on that manager's roster, from the week he arrived onward. Drop him and the counter stops, which is the honest read — the move stopped paying at that point.

Over Repl. is the same points measured against what a freely available player at his position would have given over those weeks. It's the fairer cross-position comparison: a tight end scoring 90 has done far more for you than a quarterback scoring the same, because the wire is always full of quarterbacks who'd match it.

Yahoo's weekly roster data records no lineup slot, so points scored on the bench count the same as points that won a matchup. A stashed breakout still shows up as a great pickup even in the weeks he never started.
