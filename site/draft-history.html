Draft History

Every player ever drafted in this league, and what each pick was worth. Search a name, then open a row to see the year-by-year breakdown.

Swipe →

Value is points scored minus what a pick at that price was expected to return, using a per-position curve fit across all four drafts. A positive number means the pick beat its cost; negative means it didn't. Total Value adds up every year a player was drafted, so a repeat bust digs a deeper hole than a one-off miss.

Points are the player's full-season total, whoever ended up rostering him — a player dropped in Week 3 still shows the whole year. Kickers are exempt from the cheap-pick allowance the other positions get, since essentially the whole position goes for $1–3 and the curve already reflects that. This covers 998 of the 1,024 picks ever made; the 26 absent are all $1–3 fliers cut before they appeared on a single weekly roster, so there's no scoring to grade them on.
